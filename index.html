01 · Citizen-to-Council Workflow

How every complaint is tracked to resolution

01

Received — Instant Reference Registration

Citizens submit maintenance details, photo evidence, municipal council selection, and exact GPS pin coordinates. A unique reference number (CSL-2026-XXXX) is issued immediately.

02

Assigned — Municipal Unit Dispatch

Council officers triage incoming cases and assign responsibility to specialized engineering, sanitation, electrical, or road maintenance divisions.

03

Updated — Transparent Field Progress

Field officers post inspection findings, scheduled repair dates, and work-in-progress logs visible in real time inside the Complaint Status Tracker.

04

Resolved — Verified Closure

Completed maintenance work is verified, logged with closure notes, and archived for municipal performance auditing.
