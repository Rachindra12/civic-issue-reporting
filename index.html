01 · Organization Overview

Mission, Problem Context & Civic Impact

Clean Sri Lanka bridges citizens and local government authorities through a single accountable digital workflow—turning neighborhood observations into verified municipal repairs.

Our Mission

To build a cleaner, safer, and more sustainable Sri Lanka by giving every resident—from Colombo Municipal wards to rural Pradeshiya Sabhas—a direct, transparent channel to report infrastructure and sanitation faults.

Problem Context

Traditional paper registers and fragmented hotlines lead to duplicate reports, unclear municipal jurisdiction, lost evidence, and zero visibility for citizens waiting on road, water, waste, or streetlight repairs.

Strategic Goals

Standardize GPS-tagged intake across all 9 provinces, route complaints to the exact Municipal or Urban Council within minutes, protect whistleblowers via anonymous reporting, and uphold a 48-hour initial dispatch SLA.
